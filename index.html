Who I Am

Focused on clean builds, readable interfaces, and dependable results.

I'm Josh Michael Damien W. Manalo, a freelance Full-Stack Web Developer (Frontend-Focused) based in the Philippines, passionate about building seamless and engaging digital experiences.
