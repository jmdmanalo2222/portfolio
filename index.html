01 Services

What I Can Help With

Plain-English services with clear deliverables.

Website Development

New marketing sites, landing pages, and small web apps built responsive, accessible, and fast from the first commit.

Deliverables
  • Responsive, accessible pages that match the agreed design
  • Source code in a Git repository you own
  • Deployment to your hosting (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or your server)
  • Handover notes covering how to update content

Website Repairs

Broken layouts, slow pages, console errors, contact forms that stopped working, or security updates you've been putting off.

Deliverables
  • A short diagnosis report listing root causes
  • Applied fixes committed to your repository
  • Before / after notes for anything user-visible
  • Recommendations for issues outside the agreed scope

Technical SEO Audits & Fixes

Find and fix the technical issues that stop search engines from crawling, rendering, and understanding your pages — without guaranteed-rankings sales talk.

Deliverables
  • Crawl and render checks with prioritised issue list
  • Indexing, canonical, robots, and sitemap review
  • Structured data and mobile-usability validation
  • Dated before / after measurements where available

Ongoing Maintenance

Keep things running after launch: dependency updates, backups, uptime checks, and small content or feature requests handled on a schedule.

Deliverables
  • Scheduled dependency and security updates
  • Backup verification (frequency depends on plan)
  • Change log of every update applied
  • Agreed response time for small requests
