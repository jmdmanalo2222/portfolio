Before we get started

What determines the final price?

The number of pages, design requirements, content preparation, integrations, and testing. You receive a defined scope and quote before we begin.

How do we choose the build approach?

Static pages suit focused business sites. A CMS lets you manage content. Dynamic backends and databases support accounts and workflows. An SPA can suit interactive tools, while Jamstack combines prebuilt pages with APIs. These approaches can overlap; we choose based on your needs.

Are the demos ready to take payments or confirmed bookings?

The portfolio demos show design and interaction ideas. A client website needs its own approved content, accounts, service setup, and production integrations before taking real payments or bookings.

What about delivery time and ongoing support?

We agree on a timeline after reviewing your content and features. Maintenance, future edits, and ongoing support can be scoped separately.
