What do static, CMS, and other build approaches mean?

These describe how the website works behind the scenes. They can overlap. I can recommend an approach based on how often you update content and what visitors need to do.

Static

Ready-made pages delivered to the browser. A practical choice for landing pages and business websites with occasional content changes.

Dynamic

A server and database generate content or handle actions. Useful for accounts, records, and business workflows.

CMS-based

A content management system lets your team edit pages, images, and articles through an admin area.

SPA / single-page application

An interface updates within the page as people work. Useful for dashboards and tools; it may still need a backend.

Jamstack

Prebuilt pages connect to services or APIs for selected features. Useful when fast public pages also need managed content or integrations.
